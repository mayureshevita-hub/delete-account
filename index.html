Evita Refill Delivery: Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users of the Evita Refill Delivery app, developed by Evita Softech Pvt Ltd, can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to evitasoftsolutions7@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Evita Refill Delivery Account.
  3. Include your registered username or phone number so we can verify your account.

If your account was created by your organization, you can also ask your organization's administrator to submit the request.

Requests are processed within 7 working days. You will receive a confirmation once deletion is complete.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account details (username, password, name, contact details)
  • Profile information and app preferences
  • Other personal data linked to your account

Data that may be retained

  • Records required for legal, audit, or accounting purposes may be kept for up to 90 days and then permanently deleted.
  • Anonymized data that cannot identify you may be retained.

Contact

For questions about this process, email evitasoftsolutions7@gmail.com.
